03

Projects

1. Improved forecast accuracy by 3%

The Problem

While working at Shiseido, I identified forecast gaps driven by changing business priorities and sales targets, which increased excess-inventory risk. A structured review process was needed to align Supply Chain, Brand, and Sales on corrective actions.

What I Did

  1. Established monthly reviews with Brand and Sales managers to assess business plans, sales targets, and forecast accuracy.
  2. Analyzed major forecast variances, identified root causes, and discussed necessary forecast adjustments with stakeholders.
  3. Aligned teams on corrective actions and documented key decisions, owners, and follow-up items.

Result

Improved forecast accuracy by 3% while strengthening cross-functional alignment and reducing excess-inventory risk.

2. Reduced delivery lead time from three weeks to one week

The Problem

During my time at Shiseido, a key wholesale customer requested delivery within one week rather than the standard three weeks, despite a multi-step process and limited warehouse capacity.

What I Did

  1. Confirmed the vessel arrival, container unloading, trucking, and warehouse receiving schedules in advance.
  2. Coordinated with Logistics and the warehouse to prioritize receiving and labeling, adjusting other product schedules when needed.
  3. Aligned QA inspection timing and closely monitored each step to keep execution on schedule.

Result

Successfully delivered a US$300K order for a key wholesale customer within one week, cutting the standard lead time by two weeks despite limited capacity.

3. Standardized 30+ planning SOPs from scratch

The Problem

When I joined Shiseido, onboarding relied heavily on verbal knowledge transfer, with no centralized training materials or documented workflows.

What I Did

  1. Collaborated with the project lead to identify and prioritize 30+ recurring planning processes.
  2. Documented workflows, checks, exceptions, and key considerations.
  3. Built a shared SOP library for onboarding and daily reference.

Result

Created 30+ standardized guidelines, supported 2 new planners, and equipped 15+ team members with a shared reference.
